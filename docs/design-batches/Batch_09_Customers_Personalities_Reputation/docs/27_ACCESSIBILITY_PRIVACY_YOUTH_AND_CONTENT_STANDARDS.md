# 27 — Accessibility, Inclusive Design, Privacy and Player Protection

**Batch 9 status:** proposal · Not deployed · Cross-batch compatibility required.

## Accessibility baseline
Readable customer names and drink targets in text; distinct drink silhouettes; focusable cards and accessible buttons; sufficient contrast for order quantities; reduced motion; independent voice/music/SFX volume; subtitles for spoken lines; warnings announced by text and not color alone. Input methods should not require precise gestures to read a customer order.

## Ethical customer design
No targeting or segmentation of **real players** by inferred ethnicity, gender, religion, disability, medical condition, financial vulnerability or precise location. Fictional guest roles and preferences are narrative metadata only. A customer being a VIP is not a real-world demographic judgment.

## Retention
Recurring guest narratives should be friendly, not obligation loops. No lost relationships for missed daily logins, irreversible negative reviews for real-life absence, or manipulative "your friend is disappointed" push notifications. Player can hide the journal and mute dialogue without losing progress.

## Data privacy
Store only required progress and interaction receipts; redact sensitive identity data in analytics. Avoid persistent raw chat logs because this baseline uses authored dialogue. Support account deletion workflows that handle relationships/entitlements according to product retention policy and applicable law, and mark downstream projections for erasure where appropriate.

## QA
VoiceOver/TalkBack smoke checks (owner directed for manual UI); keyboard and color-vision checks; locale pseudotranslation; text zoom; child-safety content review; replay UI consistency; consent restrictions for marketing notifications.
