# 19 — Chapter Pacing, Mission Density and Difficulty Planning

## Long-term scale without content bloat
The ambition is hundreds of missions per major island, not hundreds of copy-paste nodes. First establish a small, verified Coconut Cove progression. Each mission must teach, test, combine or remix a distinct decision. Mission density should be driven by engagement data and authoring capacity.

## Proposed first island arc (future content targets)
| Chapter | Design theme | Initial target nodes | Complexity rule |
|---|---|---:|---|
| First Steps Pier | Tutorial, basic merges and orders | 5–7 | One new mechanic at a time |
| Palm Boardwalk | Aim, rebound, managing multiple orders | 6–9 | Clear consequences, forgiving board |
| Reef Lookout | Optional fork, bank shot, first hidden clue | 6–10 | Introduce choices gradually |
| Harbor Gate | Combo, mastery challenge, gateway | 5–8 | Celebrate learned skills |
These are authoring targets; do not label nodes playable until a matching real mission exists and has golden-run proof.

## Difficulty curve ownership
Batch 4 defines physics-mode difficulty and star metrics. Batch 5 chooses *ordering*, *dependencies*, *teaching progression*, and *visibility*. Do not add a second independent scoring formula at the map layer. The difficulty tag on a node is editorial metadata and should track observed clear/retry data.

## Campaign pacing
Recommend a short onboarding series, an early completion celebration, a first branch after the core loop is learned, and an accessible gateway preview. Optional paths add depth without delaying mainline. Avoid placing several precision-only stages consecutively or forcing a high-star gate for beginners.

## Difficulty instrumentation
Per node and version: entry rate, win rate, median attempts-to-first-clear, time to clear, star distribution, abandon rate, crash/error rate, fallback/help usage. Watch for cohorts with unexpectedly high loss or unreachable mobile targets. Avoid altering a published mission silently; tune as a new mission version after testing.

## Difficulty shock checks
Compare next mission complexity to previous available missions. Large jumps should be telegraphed and/or placed on optional mastery branches. If branches eventually reconverge, ensure the convergence mission does not assume a skill taught only on the optional path.

## Release-specific content gate
For first map release, quality beats count. Ship 6–12 *fully verified* missions if 20–40 cannot be authored and tested. The long-term target of 100–300 per island is not a reason to ship broken placeholder buttons.
