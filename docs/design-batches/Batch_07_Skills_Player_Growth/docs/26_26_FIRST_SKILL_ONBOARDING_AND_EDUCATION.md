# 26 — Tutorial, First Point and Progressive Disclosure

**Batch 7 design status:** proposed · Not shipped · Interfaces and tuning subject to owner approval.

## First encounter
When trusted progression awards the first point, show a short moment after the mission result and before returning to the map: `You earned a Skill Point`. Explain that points improve your casual experience; competitive challenges use equalized rules. Offer `Choose a skill` and `Later` actions.

## Guided first purchase
- Highlight two contrasting starter talents (one information perk, one hospitality/economy perk when supported).
- Preview exactly what the next rank provides, including disabled modes and bounded probabilities.
- Require explicit tap/click to allocate; do not auto-spend first point to chase activation metrics.
- Confirm with server and update UI only after acceptance.
- Allow the player to revisit and try a new path via affordable/free respec policy.

## Return players and migrations
If an account gains the first point due to a migration, show a one-time changelog and a nonblocking CTA, not the entire beginner tutorial. Track completion per tutorial version. Don't play unskippable intro animations repeatedly on reconnect.

## Build identity copy
Use grounded language: `Hospitality improves your chances of attracting VIP visitors` rather than `always get rich customers`. Avoid exaggerated odds and hidden gambling framing.
