# 06 — Specializations, Build Archetypes and Hybrid Play

**Batch 7 design status:** proposed · Not shipped · Interfaces and tuning subject to owner approval.

## Six nonexclusive branches
| Branch ID | Fantasy | Primary effects | What it never controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mixologist` | Master the craft | UI insight, personal combo feedback, recipe mastery | Baseline physics and tier evolution |
| `hospitality` | Attract and delight guests | Tips, loyalty, VIP odds | Guaranteed rich customer without explicit product policy |
| `mogul` | Grow efficient businesses | Renovation discount, operations transparency | Minting funds, altering payout receipts |
| `explorer` | See the wider archipelago | Optional hints, discovery quality-of-life | Mandatory campaign gate bypass |
| `risk` | Prepare and recover | Forecasting, insurance explanation, recovery buffers | Forced debt, punitive event probabilities |
| `puzzle` | Plan technical shots | Casual-mode previews and tool capacity | Ranked physics or standardized challenge score |

## Build identity is descriptive, not a hard class
Assign a player-facing archetype from weighted invested ranks. If investments tie, show `Versatile` rather than arbitrarily changing the class. An optional primary-banner selection is presentation only. Players may invest across all six trees subject to points and prerequisites.

## Hybrid build examples
- `Host Mixologist`: hospitality tips + mixologist feedback; stronger casual earnings but no ranked advantage.
- `Explorer Mogul`: clearer hidden-path hints + renovation planning; cannot reveal unpublished islands.
- `Risk-Aware Specialist`: event forecast + customer moderation; reduced unpleasant surprises without removing all consequences.
- `Puzzle Analyst`: trajectory information + next-item peek in casual mode; receives standardized assistance in ranked mode.

## Build differentiation tests
Simulate multiple profiles at the same progression and content seed. Track currency velocity, VIP frequency, successful clears and disabled-mode notices. A build with only one overwhelmingly optimal choice is a balance failure. Define intentional benefits and opportunity costs, not hidden penalties.

## Seasonal cosmetics
Titles, profile cards and banners may reflect investment but do not establish permanent leaderboard superiority. No exclusive permanent physics perk for a paid season pass.
