# 13 — Expansion, Property, Franchises and Business Empire

**Status:** Batch 6 design/engineering handoff · 2026-11-14 · Proposed, not shipped.

## Expansion thesis
Long-term objectives should include saving to open new shops in different island districts, specializing each business, and building a recognizable brand. A player may prioritize efficiency, luxury tourism or visual spectacle. Never force everyone into the same optimal franchise sequence.

## Property state
`locationId` is authored by Batch 5 island graph. `playerShopId` tracks actual owned premise. Properties have unlock requirements, acquisition cost, archetype eligibility, upgrade slots, status, unique operating restrictions and appearance variants.

## Example expansion flow
See locked market stall on map → complete required mission → receive *unlock entitlement* → inspect actual purchase price and expected benefits → pay via trusted wallet transaction → own new shop → customize shop → select active operating shop where a mission allows.

## Capacity controls
Potential limits: active shops by player level, diminishing revenue returns, per-island ownership restrictions, and caps on passive income. All require measured balancing and must not shut down existing customers after unrelated progression changes.

## Deferred mechanics
Land auctions, leasing, franchising, asset resale, player-to-player marketplaces, equity, and guild-owned buildings need independent fraud, licensing, economic and abuse reviews. Do not implement them as hidden v0.3 functionality.
