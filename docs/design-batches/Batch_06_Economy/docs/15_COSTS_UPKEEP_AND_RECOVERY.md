# 15 — Operating Costs, Maintenance and Anti-Punishment Policies

**Status:** Batch 6 design/engineering handoff · 2026-11-14 · Proposed, not shipped.

## Baseline
**No compulsory recurring rent, taxes, salaries, power bills or maintenance in v0.3**. Early progression should not turn into an obligation simulator. All future upkeep policies require opt-in/clear anticipation, an affordable recovery path and finite exposure.

## Deferred upkeep examples
Optional equipment servicing to preserve a temporary buff; short-lived marketing campaigns; chosen staffing contracts; periodic supply orders; expansion permits. A repair mission is an alternate gameplay path, not simply a penalty screen.

## Recovery guarantees
- Preserve at least one playable starter shop and free core mission path.
- Never lock the last remaining income-producing mission behind a required purchase.
- If an optional shop is closed, other game systems remain available.
- Absent players cannot lose all durable assets because of elapsed time alone.
- Emergency options include bounded repair quests, installment plans, and no-cost gradual recovery.

## Negative-state testing
Simulate players at 0 coins, 1 coin, no active upgrades, multiple optional shops, expired boosts, intermittent access and out-of-order event results. A valid path back to functional play must always exist.
