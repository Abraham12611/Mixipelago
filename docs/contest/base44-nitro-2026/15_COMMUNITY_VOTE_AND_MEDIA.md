# 15 — Launchpad Community, Outreach and Media

## Community track mechanics

According to official terms, the **top 25 eligible submissions by valid Launchpad upvotes** at end of voting make the community shortlist. The Base44-appointed panel then judges the shortlisted games against gameplay, creativity and technical execution. Upvotes **do not directly select cash-prize winners**.

## Explicit prohibitions

No bots, scripts, artificial/duplicate accounts, bought votes, incentives, rewards for voting, vote exchanges or schemes that pressure users to vote. Do not pay creators for votes or coordinate "vote for me, I'll vote for you" campaigns. Do not award in-game coins, skins, access or anything of value for an upvote.

## Allowed approach

- Share truthful gameplay clips on personal channels and game-dev communities where posting is permitted.
- Invite people to **try the game** and give candid feedback; mention that a Launchpad upvote is available if they genuinely enjoy it.
- Share one clear Base44 link and one Launchpad listing; avoid misleading trackers and shortlinks.
- Post development updates with authentic screenshots/recorded runs, not AI mockups represented as live play.
- Ask for organic suggestions and recognize players **without tying any reward to votes**.
- Use good descriptions and community rules; avoid spam and unsolicited mass messaging.

## Media kit

- Game title and two-sentence hook.
- One square cover and one 16:9 image.
- 15–45 second actual gameplay clip: aim → rebound → merge → auto-serve → win/map.
- GIF/muted loop only if captured from the shipped game.
- 3–4 screenshots: home, active play, merge/serve moment, island map.
- A short FAQ: free, guest browser game, no login, mobile + desktop, where to play.
- Asset licensing/attribution note for press use as appropriate.

## Lightweight community messages

**Launch post (adapt to platform):**

> Mixipelago: Coconut Cove is a free browser game where you shoot tropical drinks across a seaside table, merge matching glasses and serve customers. You can play instantly—no account or download. I built it for the Base44 × Nitro Web Game Contest. Here's the game: [real Base44 URL]. Feedback on aiming and difficulty is welcome.

**Follow-up (only when accurate):**

> New Coconut Cove levels and polishing updates are live before the contest cutoff. If you try it, tell me whether the collisions and customer orders feel clear on your device. If you enjoyed it, you can find the entry on Base44 Launchpad.

Never insert a placeholder into published copy; replace only with verified real links.

## Community data and privacy

Don't scrape voters, collect emails unnecessarily or build referral tracking into the contest game. Basic aggregate platform counts suffice for outreach assessment. No fake "X players online" counters.

## Schedule

Prepare assets before submission, promote from actual live URL and Launchpad listing once available. Community voting closes Dec 3 23:59 UTC; continued promotion after deadline should not imply votes still count.
